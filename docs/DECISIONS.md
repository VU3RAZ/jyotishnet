| Rectification fit significance | The best fit on the ribbon comes with a permutation test (`btr.significance`, `/v1/btr/curve` `shuffles`, default 200): the same chart is scored on sets of random dates for the same event types, each date uniform over the span of the real events widened by 5 years each side and never before birth; the statistic is the best total over the whole window (so looking at many candidate times is paid for), p = (1 + random sets at least as good) / (sets + 1), seeded from the birth and events so it is reproducible.
